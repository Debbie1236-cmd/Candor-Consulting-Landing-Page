THE PROBLEM

When ownership is missing, execution suffers.

When people wait to be told what to do, escalate every problem, work strictly within the boundaries of their job descriptions or blame circumstances when things go wrong, execution suffers.

The Candor Consulting Leadership Masterclass helps employees move from simply completing tasks to taking genuine ownership of outcomes.

Participants will learn how to take responsibility, solve problems, follow through on commitments and deliver results.

WHAT PARTICIPANTS WALK AWAY WITH

Five shifts that turn intention into execution.

Own it.

Take responsibility beyond the boundaries of your job description.

Solve it.

Solve problems before they become bigger.

Deliver it.

Turn commitments into consistent follow-through and results.

Prioritise it.

Manage competing priorities, expectations and responsibilities effectively.

Account for it.

Have the courage to take responsibility when things go wrong.

THE PROGRAMME EXPERIENCE

This is not another lecture on accountability.

This is a practical, highly interactive masterclass built around realistic workplace situations and the challenges professionals face every day.

  • Examine common ownership gaps in the workplace.
  • Work through realistic workplace situations and case studies.
  • Identify behaviours that limit accountability and execution.
  • Practise practical approaches to taking ownership.
  • Develop actionable commitments they can apply immediately.
